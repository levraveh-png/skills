# Image credits and replacement guide

None of these are real AMPM Glass and Board Up job photos — none existed
in the repository to use. They are free-to-use (Unsplash License, no
attribution required) real photography, chosen to match the site's
professional/trade direction until real job and team photography is
available. Replace them with real photos as soon as possible; keep the
same filenames and aspect ratios and nothing else needs to change.

| File | Used on | Replace with |
|---|---|---|
| `glass-hero.*` | Home hero, Glass Replacement / Storefronts hero | A real photo of a completed storefront glass or board-up job |
| `glass-interior-detail.*` | Glass Replacement page | A real interior/detail shot of glass you've installed |
| `glass-detail.*` | Glass Replacement page, accent | A close-up of glass or hardware from a real job |
| `frameless-shower.*` | Frameless shower section | A real completed frameless shower glass installation |
| `service-area-la.*` | Service area section | Optional — a real neighborhood/job-site photo from the LA area you serve |

Each image ships as both `.jpg` (broad-compatibility fallback) and `.webp`
(smaller, used first via `<picture>`), same filename otherwise.

No real photo was available for the Board-Up Services hero — a boarded
storefront looks alarming as a first impression, so that page leans on
typography and the `glass-hero` image rather than damage photography.
Swap in a real "after" board-up photo when one is available.
